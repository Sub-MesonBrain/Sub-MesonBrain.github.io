  <h1>Research in public.</h1>
  <p class="hero-copy">Quant Notes is a personal notebook for exploring how markets work and how trading systems are built.</p>
</section>
<section class="prose about-prose">
  <h2>What you'll find here</h2>
  <p>Technical breakdowns of quantitative trading ideas, Web3 market mechanics, trading costs, protocol design, and software infrastructure. The goal is to show assumptions, methods, and limitations—not just conclusions.</p>
  <h2>How to read these posts</h2>
  <p>Posts are independent analysis, not investment advice. Fees, contract behavior, and protocol settings can change. Where possible, articles link to primary sources and identify assumptions that need independent verification.</p>
</section>
